Balloon Challenge

My first Scratch game. No AI has been used to build it. I know it is a simple task, but it really helped me with the pragmatic understanding of Loops, conditionals, variables, functions...Not bragging here, but celebrating my first small win...

In this game, you click a moving balloon with your mouse to score points. The balloon gets faster as your score increases. You have 5 seconds to pop the balloon before the game ends.

You'll then have to click the green flag to start or restart the game...
